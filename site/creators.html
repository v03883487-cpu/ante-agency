How to join Antefluence

A transparent guide for streamers and creators: who we look for, how onboarding works and exactly what share we take.

Requirements

Who we look for

We work with creators who have a real audience and want long-term partnerships with iGaming brands.

  • Regular streams or content — at least weekly
  • A real, engaged audience — no bought views or followers
  • An audience in the markets we cover
  • Twitch, Kick, YouTube or another major platform
  • Openness to iGaming content for an 18+ audience
Step by step

How to get in

  1. Apply

    Send us links to your channels and basic stats: average viewers, audience GEO, platforms.

  2. Review

    We check your audience, engagement and content fit with our brands.

  3. Onboarding

    You sign a contract and get a personal manager who handles brands for you.

  4. First campaign

    We match you with a brand that fits your audience and launch the first integration.
