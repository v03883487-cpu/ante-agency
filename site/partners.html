Which payout models do you offer?

CPA, RevShare and Hybrid. The model and the rate depend on the offer and the GEO.

Can I use my own tracker?

Yes. We support S2S postbacks for Keitaro, Binom, Voluum, RedTrack, BeMob and any tracker that accepts a postback URL.

Can I run other networks' offers through you?

Yes. Other networks send conversions to our endpoint and we pass them on to your tracker, so all your statistics stay in one place.

How do payouts work?

Through our own payment system: crypto or fiat, on a schedule agreed when you start.

Which traffic is not allowed?

Incentivized traffic, brand bidding without approval, fraud and any traffic to audiences under 18.
