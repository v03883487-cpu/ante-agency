What it measures

Every number that
matters for your budget

CTRClick-through rate — how many viewers click a creator's link or promo code.
CPACost per action — what one registration or first deposit costs you.
CACCustomer acquisition cost — the full cost of bringing in a paying player.
ROASReturn on ad spend — revenue earned for every dollar invested.
ConversionsRegistrations, first deposits and repeat deposits by creator and GEO.
CPMCost per thousand views — how efficiently a stream or video buys reach.
EPCEarnings per click — deposits divided by clicks on creator links.
ROIReturn on investment — the campaign's profit against its total cost.
