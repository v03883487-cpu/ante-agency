Insights

What we've learned
from hundreds of streams

Short, practical notes for iGaming marketers who work with creators.

01

Live streams convert better than posts

A stream is a conversation in real time: the creator answers questions, shows the product and repeats the promo code while viewers are already engaged. That's why a live integration usually beats a single post with the same reach.

02

Choose creators by audience GEO, not follower count

A smaller creator whose audience lives in your licensed markets will bring more depositing players than a big channel with viewers from everywhere. Always ask for the audience breakdown by country.

03

Measure CAC, not views

Views and CPM look nice in a report, but the budget decision should rest on what a paying player costs you. Track every creator through their own link or promo code from day one.

04

Series beat one-offs

Audiences trust a brand they see again and again on the same channel. A series of integrations with one creator usually outperforms the same budget spread across many single placements.

05

Compliance comes first

Advertising rules for gambling differ from GEO to GEO: age gates, disclaimers, banned wording. Build them into the brief before the creator goes live — fixing it afterwards costs more.
